[#66 Conceptual] 

The 0-Sphere model fixes the internal speed of the electron at \(v_{\mathrm{ZB}}=0.04047\,c\) from the anomalous magnetic moment alone, while the Dirac equation attaches the speed \(c\) to the Zitterbewegung through the eigenvalues \(\pm c\) of its velocity operator. This Letter answers the resulting objection by separating two meanings of \(c\): the invariant speed of the Lorentz group, which the model keeps without modification, and the group velocity of the internal carrier, which it sets to \(c/n\) with \(n=1/\beta_{\mathrm{ZB}}=24.71\), in the manner of light in water. Fizeau's experiment shows that such a medium-internal speed is relativistically admissible, and the Foldy–Wouthuysen representation shows that the Dirac eigenvalues do not fix it. The index is assigned, not derived. Two appendices explain why a group velocity belongs in the model: the energy identity moves the energy from one kernel to the other gradually, so that each transfer is a wave group, continuous in phase and discrete in frequency.

— Core Equations —

Two meanings of \(c\): invariant speed kept, carrier slowed (Sec. II):

\[ v_{\mathrm{ZB}}=\frac{c}{n},\qquad n=\frac{1}{\beta_{\mathrm{ZB}}}=24.708,\qquad \beta_{\mathrm{ZB}}^2=0.00163798087 \]

Fizeau drag of the internal carrier (Sec. III):

\[ u\simeq\frac{c}{n}+v\left(1-\frac{1}{n^2}\right),\qquad 1-\frac{1}{n^2}=1-\beta_{\mathrm{ZB}}^2=0.998362 \]

The Dirac eigenvalues belong to one position operator (Foldy–Wouthuysen, Sec. IV):

\[ \dot{\boldsymbol x}=c\,\boldsymbol\alpha\ \ (\text{eigenvalues }\pm c),\qquad \dot{\boldsymbol X}=\frac{c^2\boldsymbol p}{E} \]

Internal index in the optical metric, and the shape-independent critical angle (Secs. V–VI):

\[ \mathcal G_{\mu\nu}=n^2(T)\,g_{\mu\nu},\qquad n(T_{\mathrm{int}})=\frac{1}{\beta_{\mathrm{ZB}}},\qquad \sin\theta_c=\beta_{\mathrm{ZB}},\ \ \theta_c=2.32^\circ \]

The energy identity resolved into frequencies (Appendix B):

\[ \frac{T_A}{E_0}=\frac38+\frac12\cos\theta+\frac18\cos2\theta,\qquad \frac{1}{E_0}\left(\frac{dT_B}{d\theta}-\frac{dT_A}{d\theta}\right)=\sin\theta,\qquad n_g=2n(2\omega)-n(\omega) \]

— Key Contributions —

  • The conflict becomes a distinction: The \(c\) of the Dirac equation is the invariant speed of spacetime; the \(0.04047\,c\) of the model is a group velocity inside the electron, as \(c/1.33\) is the speed of light in water. Lorentz covariance, spin and \(g=2\) are untouched.
  • Two independent precedents: Fizeau's moving-water experiment, reproduced by the relativistic composition of velocities, shows that a medium selects a rest frame while every observer keeps the invariant speed; the electron is a moving medium with drag coefficient \(1-\beta_{\mathrm{ZB}}^2\). The Foldy–Wouthuysen mean position moves at \(c^2\boldsymbol p/E\) without trembling, so the eigenvalues \(\pm c\) are tied to one choice of position operator, not to a carrier speed.
  • The number acquires a place: In the optical metric \(n^2(T)\,g\) established in Paper #51, \(\beta_{\mathrm{ZB}}\) is the reciprocal of the internal index. The question why the internal speed is about \(0.04\,c\) becomes the question why the internal index is about \(25\); \(n^2=610.51\) agrees with \(\sqrt2\,\pi/\alpha\) to \(0.28\%\). The two internal clocks of Paper #65 are related by the same index, \(\nu_C/\nu_{\mathrm{ZB}}=n\).
  • A consequence that needs no shape: Wherever the index returns to unity, total internal reflection sets in above \(\theta_c=2.32^\circ\), whatever the shape of the internal region. Shape-dependent quantities (confinement, leakage) are left to a later paper.
  • Why a group velocity: The energy identity \(\cos^4(\theta/2)+\sin^4(\theta/2)+\tfrac12\sin^2\theta=1\) does not move the energy at once. The radiation gradient is exactly the difference of the derivatives of the two kernel energies, \(\sin\theta\), which vanishes at the start and end of each transfer; a transfer of constant amplitude is excluded, and each transfer is a wave group whose envelope is fixed by the identity and restored at every cycle.
  • Continuous in phase, discrete in frequency: The identity balances separately at the constant, at \(\omega\) (the exchange between the kernels) and at \(2\omega\) (the exchange between the kernels and the photon sphere). The spectrum stops at \(2\omega\) because the energy is the square of the share, and the group index of two lines is the difference quotient \(n_g=2n(2\omega)-n(\omega)\).

— Empirical Prediction —

  • Competing frequencies for the same phenomenon: \(\nu_{\mathrm{ZB}}=\beta_{\mathrm{ZB}}c/\lambda_C=5.00\times10^{18}\) Hz (photon energy 20.7 keV) for the 0-Sphere model against \(2m_ec^2/h=2.47\times10^{20}\) Hz (1.022 MeV) for the Dirac Zitterbewegung. Trapped-ion simulations reproduce the simulated Hamiltonian by construction and do not decide between them; a direct X-ray search for a resonant signature of the free electron does.
