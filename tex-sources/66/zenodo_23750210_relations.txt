[1] Continues
  — Papers whose central results this paper takes over and extends —

  #10 Hanamura 2024 — Redefining Electron Spin and Anomalous Magnetic Moment
      Through Harmonic Oscillation and Lorentz Contraction
      DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17764997
      Reason: #10 fixes the internal speed v_ZB = 0.04047 c from the anomalous
              magnetic moment alone (Input I1). #66 answers the objection that
              this value contradicts the speed c of the Dirac equation, by
              reading it as the group velocity c/n of an internal carrier with
              n = 1/beta_ZB = 24.71 (Sec. II, Results R1-R2).

  #51 Hanamura 2026 — Helical Trajectory, Fixed-Endpoint Line Integrals, and
      the Emergence of the Spacetime Metric in the 0-Sphere Model
      DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20388056
      Reason: #51 establishes, as an identity, that the thermal metric is the
              optical metric G = n^2(T) g (Input I2), and fixes the round-trip
              path lambda_C of one transport cycle (Input I3). #66 assigns the
              value n(T_int) = 1/beta_ZB within that metric (Sec. V, R5) and
              draws the shape-independent consequences (Sec. VI, R6).

  #65 Hanamura 2026 — From Zero and One to the Electron: Path-Connectedness,
      the Double Cover, and the Geometric Origin of the Quartic Energy in the
      0-Sphere Model
      DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23257458
      Reason: Immediate predecessor. #65 separates two internal rates (the
              light-speed reference c/lambda_C and the transport frequency
              nu_ZB) and fixes the quartic energy as the square of the share.
              #66 relates the two rates by the index, nu_C / nu_ZB = n
              (App. A.2, B.3), and resolves the quartic energies into a
              constant, a fundamental and a second harmonic (App. B.4-B.5,
              Results R8-R9).
