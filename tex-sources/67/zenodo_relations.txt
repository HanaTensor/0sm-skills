[1] Continues
  — Papers whose central results this paper takes over and extends —

  #51 Hanamura 2026 — Helical Trajectory, Fixed-Endpoint Line Integrals, and
      the Emergence of the Spacetime Metric in the 0-Sphere Model (version 3)
      DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24521440
      Reason: #51 v3 reads the dynamical phase S_dyn = omega_ZB * integral of
              d tau as a Hamilton principal function and builds the world
              function from it (Input I2): the ray layer of Hamilton's
              analogy. #67 takes the step from that ray layer to the wave
              layer (Secs. II-III, Result R1) and evaluates the dynamical
              phase over one round trip (Sec. IV, R3). The reading of the
              factor beta_ZB that #51 v3 leaves open is restated, not
              derived (Sec. IX).

  #66 Hanamura 2026 — Subluminal Zitterbewegung and the Invariant Speed: An
      Internal Refractive Index for the 0-Sphere Model
      DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23750210
      Reason: Immediate predecessor. #66 assigns the internal index
              n = 1/beta_ZB, identifying phase and group indices, and resolves
              the energy identity into the lines 0, omega, 2 omega (Input I3).
              #67 separates the two indices (Sec. IV, R2), states what the
              closure of the cycle requires of the carrier (R3), places the
              discrete group velocity in the line of the correspondence
              principle (Sec. VII, R6), and qualifies the use of Snell's law
              and the critical angle (Sec. IV).
